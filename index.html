How to play

Make every face of the cube a single colour.

  • Drag on a sticker to turn that row or column.
  • Drag on the background to spin the whole cube and look around.
  • Scroll / pinch to zoom.
  • Keys: U D L R F B turn a face clockwise, hold Shift to turn it anticlockwise, Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes.

Press Scramble to start. The timer starts on your first move.
